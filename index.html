About the Journal

International Journal of Ayurveda and Pharmaceutical Research (IJAPR) is a scholarly publication associated with V-Enlight Pharmacy Academy.

The journal provides a platform for researchers, academicians, pharmacy professionals, healthcare professionals and scholars to communicate research relating to Ayurveda, pharmaceutical sciences, pharmacy practice, healthcare and related disciplines.

Original research articles, review articles and other scholarly contributions may be submitted for consideration according to the journal's editorial requirements.
